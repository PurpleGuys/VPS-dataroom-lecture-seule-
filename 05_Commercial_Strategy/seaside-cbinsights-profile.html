Seaside Equity Partners Launches Environmental Services Platform, Enviracore Services™

Sep 30, 2025

announce the launch of Enviracore Services, LLC (“Enviracore” or the “Company”), a platform investment in the environmental services industry. Enviracore was formed with the vision of building the next industry-leading provider of environmental services, emergency response, industrial cleaning, and wastewater treatment Enviracore was formed with the vision of building the next industry-leading provider of environmental services, emergency response, industrial cleaning, and wastewater treatment services to commercial, industrial, and municipal clients throughout the United States. By aligning with organizations that share similar values and investing in distinctive talent, service, and operations, Enviracore will seek to become a trusted service provider to its broad base of customers. “There is a clear opportunity in the current market for Enviracore to emerge as the leading provider of environmental services,” said Tommy Crenshaw, Chief Executive Officer of Enviracore. “We intend to become a market leader by committing to our core values and delivering high quality service. Ultimately, this will enhance the experience for our valued customers and employees.” Enviracore's first two partner companies, Boomer Environmental and Environmental Cleanup, Inc., will continue to operate under their respective brands, delivering environmental services, industrial cleaning, wastewater treatment, and emergency response services to the Oklahoma and Arkansas markets. About Enviracore Services Enviracore is a market leader in environmental services with a network of top-tier environmental brands and premier operational infrastructure. By sharing resources, best practices, expertise, and values, Enviracore delivers top-notch environmental, industrial cleaning, wastewater treatment, and emergency response services to customers across an expanding footprint. For additional information on Enviracore Services™, see enviracoreservices.com About Seaside Equity Partners Seaside Equity Partners is a growth-oriented and operationally focused private equity firm based in San Diego, CA. The firm seeks to provide capital, resources, and strategic support to leading mission critical services businesses that are headquartered in the Western United States. With nearly $1.4 billion in AUM as of June 30, 2025, Seaside is actively pursuing new opportunities to partner with founder, family, and entrepreneur-owned businesses. Compare Seaside Equity Partners to Competitors

S
Salt Creek Capital

Salt Creek Capital is a private investment firm that provides capital solutions and operational leadership for ownership transitions in various industries. The firm partners with entrepreneurs and family-owned businesses and has an Executive Partnership Program to assist operators in value creation. Salt Creek Capital's investment portfolio includes business and consumer services, manufacturing, food & beverage, franchising, healthcare, and distribution sectors. It was founded in 2009 and is based in Redwood City, California.

Century Park Capital Partners

Century Park Capital Partners is a private equity firm focused on collaborating with owners and managers to develop middle market companies. The firm specializes in facilitating liquidity for owners and supporting growth through the addition of industry and operational resources, both organically and through acquisitions. Century Park primarily invests in profitable, owner-operated businesses across various sectors including business services, specialty manufacturing, and consumer products and services. Century Park Capital Partners was formerly known as CPC Pivotal Drive Holdings, LLC. It was founded in 1999 and is based in El Segundo, California.

Red Barn Equity Partners

Red Barn Equity Partners is a private investment firm focused on lower middle market assets in the consumer, industrial, business services, and healthcare industries. The company provides capital support and strategic guidance to its portfolio companies. Red Barn Equity Partners primarily serves family offices and high net worth individuals. It was founded in 2019 and is based in West Palm Beach, Florida.

Prairie Capital

Prairie Capital is a private equity fund focused on investing in lower middle market companies across various sectors. The company provides control equity investments to founder-owned businesses, offering capital and expertise to help them grow. Prairie Capital primarily serves sectors such as business services, consumer, education, healthcare, industrial, and technology. It was founded in 1997 and is based in Chicago, Illinois.

Bessemer Investors

Bessemer Investors is a company that focuses on private equity, specifically in the middle-market business sector. The company's main service is to partner with these businesses to support their growth and enhance value creation, utilizing a long-term, flexible capital base and a team of experienced private equity professionals. Bessemer Investors primarily serves the private equity industry. It was founded in 2018 and is based in New York, New York.

DFW Capital Partners

DFW Capital Partners operates as a private equity investment firm focused on the lower middle-market across various sectors. The company partners with business owners and management teams to support growth and strategic initiatives. DFW Capital Partners primarily invests in management buyouts, corporate divestitures, and liquidity-driven recapitalizations, and provides capital for acquisitions. It was founded in 1983 and is based in New York, New York.
