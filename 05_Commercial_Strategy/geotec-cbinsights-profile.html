About Geotec

Geotec specializes in geotechnical and environmental engineering within the construction and infrastructure sectors. The company offers a comprehensive range of services including soil studies, structural diagnostics, natural risk analysis, geothermal energy assessments, geophysical exploration, and maritime project consulting. Geotec also designs and manufactures its own range of drilling equipment. It was founded in 1973 and is based in Chevigny-Saint-Sauver, France.

Murray Rix

Murray Rix focuses on geotechnical engineering and construction materials testing within the civil engineering and construction sectors. The company provides ground engineering operations, site investigations, laboratory services accredited by UKAS, and consultancy support. Murray Rix serves clients in the construction, civil engineering, and quarrying industries. It was founded in 1994 and is based in England, United Kingdom.

Tolunay-Wong Engineers

Tolunay-Wong Engineers is a firm specializing in geotechnical engineering within the civil engineering sector. The company offers a range of services including subsurface exploration, foundation design recommendations, construction materials testing, and near-surface geophysical explorations. Tolunay-Wong Engineers primarily serves the residential, commercial, infrastructure, and environmental sectors. It was founded in 1993 and is based in Houston, Texas.

G
Geoconsult

Geoconsult is a company that provides engineering and consulting services for infrastructure projects in different sectors. The company offers services including geotechnical investigations, foundation engineering, construction management, and tunnel construction, along with digital services like geographic information systems and building information modeling. Geoconsult serves sectors that require infrastructure development and maintenance, such as civil engineering, mining, and electrical engineering. It was founded in 1973 and is based in Salzburg, Austria.

N
Northwest Geotech

Northwest Geotech conducts geotechnical and geo-environmental investigations within the site investigation service sector. The company provides services including site investigations, soil testing, foundation design, and reporting, focused on assessments and evaluations of ground conditions. Northwest Geotech serves sectors that require understanding of geotechnical and environmental conditions for their projects. It was founded in 1983 and is based in Derry, Northern Ireland.

Riken Geology

Riken Geology specializes in geotechnical and geological surveys within the construction and civil engineering sectors. The company provides soil testing, ground penetration tests, and geological assessments to determine soil characteristics and ground stability services essential for ensuring the safety and viability of construction projects. It was founded in 1983 and is based in Kanazawa, Japan.

The company has products including systems for structural health monitoring, material characterization, and non-destructive testing, as well as software solutions for data acquisition and analysis. Boviar serves sectors that require safety and monitoring solutions, such as construction, infrastructure management, and environmental protection. It was founded in 1968 and is based in Milan, Italy.
