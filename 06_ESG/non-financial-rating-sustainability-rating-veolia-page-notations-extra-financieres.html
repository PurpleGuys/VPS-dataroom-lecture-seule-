Sustainability rating

Veolia is rated for its CSR performance based on its published information and statements.

Global ESG ratings

Veolia Environnement is included, among others, in the Dow Jones Best-in-Class World and Europe Indices (formerly DJSI World and Europe), as well as in the FTSE4Good and CAC40 ESG stock market indices.

As of 22/09/2025, Veolia Environnement was ranked in the first decile of the Multi-Utilities industry by ISS-ESG, achieving a score of B.

 As of 09/07/2026, Veolia Environnement scored 81/100 in the S&P Global Corporate Sustainability Assessment, and was ranked in the 97th percentile in the MUW Multi and Water Utilities industry.
 

Veolia Environnement is recognized in the Top 5% of the Multi and Water Utilities industry by S&P Global, in its 2025 Sustainability Yearbook. Of the over 7,690 companies assessed, spread across 62 industries, only 780 are selected in the Yearbook.

As of 29/07/2025, Veolia Environnement was ranked in the top 1% of companies rated by Ecovadis in the Water Collection, Treatment and Supply industry, achieving a score of 82/100.

Specific ESG ratings

Veolia Environnement is on the CDP A List 2025. The Group received double recognition for its Leadership level, achieving two A scores, for CDP Climate Change and for CDP Water Security. 

Evaluation criteria

These assessments are valuable information, as they provide an independent evaluation of the company’s ESG performance along with ongoing monitoring of key sustainable development criteria.

The Group is evaluated on a range of criteria, including governance, human resources management, environmental performance, ethics, human rights, customer and supplier relations, and dialogue with civil society.

The ESG rating allows the Group to stay tuned to the experts and thereby continuously improve its sustainable development process.

Being selected in ESG indexes and being recognized by its customers is an acknowledgement of the company's long-term commitment to CSR and the quality of its performance.
