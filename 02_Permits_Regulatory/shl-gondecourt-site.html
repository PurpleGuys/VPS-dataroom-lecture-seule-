SHL in Gondecourt (59147)

Centre for the regeneration of used oils, the treatment of polluted water, the purification of heat transfer fluids and mineral oils, energy recovery and reformulation to technical specifications.

  • Its activities are authorised by the Prefectoral Decree of 06/01/2010
  • Authorized for the following capacities :
  • Hydrocarbon and mineral oil residues: 10,000 tons for regenerable mineral oils and 30,000 tons for hydrocarbon residues
  • Vegetable and/or animal oils and grease: 30,000 tons
  • ISO 9001 version 2000, ISO 14001, ISO 45001 certifications

Why work with us ?
Keen to preserve the environment and help local businesses and organisations to contribute to this goal, ARF gives priority to materials and energy recovery in the processing of their waste.

An independent company, our autonomy gives us the necessary flexibility and proactive approach to providing reliable services with a maximum of guarantees in compliance with the environmental regulations in force.
